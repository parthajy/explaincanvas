# Play Store listing copy

**App name** (30 max)
ExplainCanvas

**Short description** (80 max)
Type any doubt. See it drawn: diagrams, steps, animation, 3D. Checked for accuracy.

**Full description** (4000 max)

Type anything you are stuck on. Understand it visually.

ExplainCanvas turns your question into the picture that teaches it: a labelled diagram, step-by-step working, slides, an animation or a 3D model you can rotate. Every explanation is written for your exact question and your level, checked for accuracy before you see it, and narrated. One quick check question at the end tells you whether it clicked.

Any subject, any level. School Science and Maths, chapter by chapter. Undergraduate engineering, economics, medicine. Postgraduate and research topics. Set your level once and every explanation is pitched there.

What you can do
• Type your doubt, dictate it, or photograph the whole homework page. The app finds every question on it and you tap the one you want.
• Get the right picture for the idea. The app decides whether a diagram, steps, an animation or a 3D model will teach it best.
• Ask for it simpler, or in Hinglish, with one tap.
• Keep going with suggested follow-up questions that take the idea one step deeper.
• Share any explanation to WhatsApp or a friend.
• Browse the free library of explanations already made and checked.

For parents
A daily email on study days: what your child understood and what still needs work, with chapter names. A Sunday recap of the week. Streak reminders. Cancel any time.

Honest about accuracy
Every fresh explanation passes a second, independent check before it is shown. Anything that fails is rewritten. If you spot a mistake, report it with one tap and it is pulled until a person has reviewed it.

Free to start
The library is free to read and every account includes one fresh explanation. Plans and credits are managed on explaincanvas.com.

ExplainCanvas is made in India, in English and Hinglish.
